## Prompt



Build a single-page responsive business website for a Ghanaian construction and carpentry company called **Samanade C.R.D Enterprise**.



### Brand

- **Name:** Samanade C.R.D Enterprise

- **Logo elements:** a crossed pair of hammers and a construction crane behind a blue-roofed house illustration, with a red ground/roof-line sweep beneath it. Colors: navy/royal blue, deep forest green (for the wordmark), red accent, black line work, white background.

- **Tone:** trustworthy, hands-on, professional but approachable — a local tradesman brand, not corporate.

- **Suggested palette:** deep forest green (#1B4D3E or similar) for headings/logo text, royal blue (#2E3A8C-ish) for the roof/graphic accents, red (#D62828-ish) for a highlight accent, white/off-white background, charcoal for body text.



### Business Description (use near-verbatim on the About/Home section)

"We provide professional carpentry, furniture fabrication, interior design, construction, repairs, and equipment/material rental services. Our work covers both local and modern designs, including custom furniture, interior fittings, doors, cabinets, and general woodwork. We are committed to quality craftsmanship, durability, timely delivery, and customer satisfaction."



### Services to list (as cards or icon list)

1. Building material rentals

2. Doors fixing / installation

3. Carpentry works (construction & furniture making)

4. Custom furniture fabrication

5. Interior design & fittings

6. Cabinets and general woodwork

7. Construction & repairs



### Site sections

1. **Header/Nav** — logo left, nav links (Home, Services, About, Contact), sticky on scroll.

2. **Hero** — logo/graphic prominent, tagline (e.g. "Quality Craftsmanship. Built to Last."), short intro line, CTA buttons: "Call Us" and "WhatsApp Us."

3. **Services** — grid of the 7 services above with simple icons.

4. **About** — the business description paragraph, emphasize local + modern design capability.

5. **Gallery/Portfolio placeholder** — a section with placeholder image blocks for future project photos (carpentry, doors, interiors) — label clearly as "add your project photos here."

6. **Contact** —

   - Phone: 0558729697 / 0543773955 (click-to-call links)

   - Location: Dodowa Bawaleshie (near King Rich Junction), Ghana

   - Social: Facebook — facebook.com/anade.samuel.10, TikTok — @anadesamuel4

   - Include a simple contact form (name, phone, message) — no backend needed, mailto or WhatsApp link fallback.

7. **Footer** — logo, social icons, copyright, phone numbers repeated.



### Functional requirements

- Fully responsive (mobile-first — most customers will find this via WhatsApp/Facebook on phone).

- Click-to-call (`tel:`) links on phone numbers.

- WhatsApp click-to-chat link using one of the numbers.

- Fast-loading, minimal dependencies, no CMS needed — static HTML/CSS/JS is fine.

- Clean, modern layout that still feels grounded/local (avoid overly generic corporate stock-photo feel).



### Assets available

- Logo image (uploaded separately) — use as the primary brand mark in the header and hero.
